About H St. Hair

Beauty That Feels Personal

Nestled in the vibrant neighborhood of H Street NE, Washington, DC, H St. Hair is a sanctuary dedicated to the art of premium hair care. We believe that exceptional hair styling goes far beyond the chair—it's about listening to your needs, understanding your hair's unique texture, and crafting a look that empowers your daily confidence.

Whether you are looking for a precision cut, transformative color, or a rejuvenating scalp treatment, our experienced stylists provide meticulous craftsmanship in an inviting, elegant atmosphere.
